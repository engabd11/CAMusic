v0.9.1 - spatial swells and UI polish
Stable build of CAMusic for Android.

A single feature and a handful of fixes.

## Spatial swells

Lights now follow sounds that move. A stereo pan, a reverb tail, or a delay throw —
anything with spatial character — lights the side of the room it came from rather
than the centre. The effect is built on the entertainment area's real 3-D positions,
with a Gaussian kernel over the room so neighbouring lamps share energy without
brightening or dimming the whole space.

## Fixes

- **Library search text** was lost when the keyboard was dismissed
- **Nine UI and library-switching fixes** from device testing, including smoother
  transitions between servers and a fix for the library grid that rebuilt on every
  scroll
