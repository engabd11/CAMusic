v0.7.0 - Light Sync straight to the Hue Bridge
Stable build of CAMusic for Android.

## Install
1. Download **camusic-0.7.0.apk** below (Android 12 / API 31+).
2. Allow **install from unknown sources**, then open it. Installs over 0.6.x (versionCode 29) — same signing key, so your servers, credentials and downloads survive.

## Light Sync without Home Assistant

Light Sync has always been Home Assistant's job here: the app drove the Hue Synco
integration, which listened to a Music Assistant player and streamed to the bridge. That
still works and is untouched. But it means lights need a server running an integration
watching a player — three things between the music and the room.

When this phone is the one playing, none of that is necessary. The audio is already here,
decoded. So the app now talks to the bridge itself.

**Settings → Servers → Light Sync** has a transport switch. Pick **Bridge (direct)**,
discover a bridge on the network, press its link button to pair, and the Lights tab
becomes a direct control surface: pick an entertainment area from the bridge, choose an
intensity and a palette, set a brightness ceiling, and turn it on. Nothing else in the
chain.

Under it: the decoded PCM is tapped out of the player's own render chain, run through an
FFT with SuperFlux onset detection and a mel filterbank, and turned into per-light colour
by a port of syncoV2's effects engine — five intensity rungs from Subtle, which holds a
steady level and just lets colour drift, up to Extreme. That goes to the bridge over DTLS
1.2 on a pre-shared key, at 60 Hz, which is what the Entertainment API expects.

## What direct mode does not do yet

It syncs **this phone's own playback** — the local player, so Navidrome and downloads.
There is no speaker to choose because the phone is the speaker. Music playing on a remote
Music Assistant player is still the Home Assistant path's job, unchanged.

Four things the Home Assistant path has and this one does not, and which are not offered
rather than offered and dead:

- **Auto intensity.** The picker that reads the music and moves between rungs is not
  ported, so direct mode asks you to choose one.
- **Album-art and song colour.** Both are derived in the integration; the direct engine
  renders its fallback palette, so only the fixed palettes are listed.
- **Effects.** Movies and Fireworks render identically to Music here.
- **Timing offset.** There is no delay buffer yet, so the lights follow the audio without
  a scheduled lead. Expect a little lag rather than beats landing dead on.

## Also

- The audio tap sits in the player's processing chain permanently rather than being
  inserted when Light Sync starts. Android builds that chain once per track, so a tap
  added later was left out until the next one — switching Light Sync on mid-song did
  nothing.
- Rendering runs on its own clock instead of firing once per analysis frame. Audio
  arrives in chunks, so the old path sent the bridge bursts and advanced the engine's
  animation by a fixed step regardless of how much time had really passed.
- The bridge connection follows playback: it opens when the phone has something playing
  and closes when it doesn't, and a pause lets the room fall dark on its own rather than
  freezing on the last frame.
