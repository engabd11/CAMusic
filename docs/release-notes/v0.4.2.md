v0.4.2 - Material 3, a Settings menu you can read, and the fixes from a week of testing
Debug build of Sendspin for Android (work in progress).

## Install
1. Download **sendspin-0.4.2-debug.apk** below (Android 12 / API 31+).
2. Allow **install from unknown sources**, then open it. Installs over 0.4.x (versionCode 25).

## What's new since v0.4.1

### Pause pauses, and seek seeks

The one that mattered. On the Music Assistant path, pressing pause left the music
playing for up to half a minute, seeking appeared to do nothing, and the only way to
stop it was the persistent notification's action - which made it look as though a
second, separate audio stream was running.

It was one bug. Music Assistant sends `stream/end` when you pause, with no
`stream/clear` alongside it, and the client advertises a 4 MB read-ahead - so the
server streams up to ~30 seconds in front. v0.4.0 changed `stream/end` to play that
buffer out instead of discarding it, to stop track tails being clipped. That is correct
for a track boundary and completely wrong for a pause, and the two messages are
byte-identical.

Pause wins. **The gapless-across-track-boundaries claim from the v0.4.0 and v0.4.1 notes
is withdrawn** on the MA path. What survives is the part that was sound: the codec and
AudioTrack are no longer rebuilt at a track change. Gapless still works on the Navidrome
path, where ExoPlayer owns the whole queue.

### Settings is a menu now

Seven headed cards on one scroll - two music servers, a home-automation token, player
identity, audio quality, downloads, layout and a version string - became seven
categories, each opening its own short list. Grouped by what you are configuring rather
than by which class implements it, which is why the Navidrome server sits with Music
Assistant and Home Assistant under **Servers**, while "which library the app browses" is
its own category. Every row says what is behind it.

### Material 3

The full six-phase M3 theme upgrade landed: colour scheme, typography, shapes, and
screen-level text routed through `MaterialTheme` instead of hand-set sizes. Stock
components now inherit the OLED look rather than needing per-call colour overrides.

### Three crashes

- **The app would not open.** `NowPlayingViewModel._radioMode` was read during
  construction by a flow declared 600 lines above it. `viewModelScope` is
  `Dispatchers.Main.immediate`, which does not defer on the main thread, so an eager
  `stateIn` runs *during* construction and anything declared below it is still null.
- **And again when the library loaded**, for the same reason, with `_frequent`.
- **And a third time** with `refreshing`, after switching backends. Fixed structurally
  rather than by moving one more property: the init block now sits at the end of the
  class, after every property exists.
- `startForegroundService` was unguarded. Android 12+ refuses it from the background and
  the refusal is an exception, thrown on a background dispatcher with nothing catching
  it.

### Switching libraries

Switching between Music Assistant and Navidrome while standing on an album left that
album on screen, backed by a server the library was no longer pointed at. A detail
screen is its own navigation destination, so clearing the library's browse stack could
never pop it. It leaves under its own power now. On the Navidrome backend the phone owns
Now Playing whether or not anything is loaded, which fixes the asymmetry where MA →
Navidrome left the MA player on screen.

### Audio and metadata

- **44.1/16 distortion on Navidrome is fixed.** `setEnableAudioFloatOutput(true)` was
  added on theory in v0.4.0; it is experimental and misbehaves on some sinks at rates
  other than the device's native one.
- **Artist About now works on Music Assistant.** MA carries the text at
  `metadata.description` and the parser was not reading it. Where MA has nothing,
  Navidrome's biography is borrowed - matched on the artist name exactly, because a
  confident biography of the wrong musician is worse than none.
- The quality card's provider line said "From Subsonic" while the player and library
  were both MA. It now says *"Music Assistant fetched this from Subsonic"*, and is
  hidden on the local player.
- Navidrome lyrics could show a completely different song's words; the legacy `getLyrics`
  endpoint can only be asked by artist and title and answers with whatever it matched.
  The response names the song it found, so it is now checked and discarded on a mismatch.
  OpenSubsonic's `offset` field is applied.
- The Now Playing volume slider on the local player is the phone's real media volume,
  read live - it used to show the MA player's level, which meant nothing there.
- Navidrome connection status in Settings now pings the server directly whichever
  backend is active, and distinguishes a rejected password from an unreachable host.
- DSP and "don't stop the music" are hidden on the Navidrome player, where they
  configure something else entirely.

### Interface

- Speaker grouping is a **switch** rather than Join/Unjoin buttons. Membership is a
  state; a button had to name the *next* state to be useful, so its label always
  contradicted the row it sat on.
- Title and subtitle pairs across the whole app share one spacing token.
- The collapsed overlay player is bigger: 48dp art, more padding, larger text. It is the
  only way back into the full player and sits directly above the nav bar.
- Em dashes are gone from every user-facing string.

## Tests

167 unit tests, lint and `assembleDebug` all pass.

## Verified on device

Launch, all tabs, album detail, Now Playing, pause, seek, library switching in both
directions, and 44.1/16 Navidrome playback were all checked on a Galaxy S23 running
Android 15 before release.

**The Material 3 typography sweep and the Settings reorganisation were not.** Both
compile and both are layout-only, but a type scale change is exactly the sort of thing
that is right in code and wrong in the eye. Worth a look at Now Playing and Settings.
