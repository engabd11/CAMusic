v0.10.1 - MA pause/seek latency and speaker badge fix
Stable build of CAMusic for Android.

Two targeted fixes for Music Assistant playback.

## MA pause/seek latency

Pause and seek on Music Assistant could take 5–12 seconds to actually go silent
or jump to the requested position. The issue was a buffer-depth mismatch: the
player was writing to a deep buffer while the seek command only flushed the
server-side state, so the already-buffered audio continued playing. Fixed by
reverting to a shallow buffer for light-sync-responsive playback.

## Speaker group badge

The group badge on the speaker card used to say "LIVE" for every member of a
group, instead of showing which speaker was driving the group (the leader). Now
the leader shows "Playing here" and members show the leader's name.
