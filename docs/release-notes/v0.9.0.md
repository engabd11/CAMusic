v0.9.0 - driving mode, the flying cover, and the widget
Stable build of CAMusic for Android.

The headline features are a driving mode, a home-screen widget, and the now-playing
cover that flies between the mini bar and the full-screen overlay.

## Driving mode

Three very large controls laid over whatever is on screen — a map, a messaging app,
anything. The default trigger is connecting to a car's Bluetooth stereo, so it shows
when the situation says so and asks for no extra setup. Picture-in-Picture is the
first mechanism; a floating overlay (with SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW permission) is the
upgrade for devices where PiP is not available.

The controls are big enough to hit without looking: play/pause, skip, and the album
art as a back button. They sit at the bottom of the screen, high enough to stay out
of the way of navigation gestures, and dim the content behind them just enough to
keep the controls readable without hiding the map.

## Now-playing cover flies

Tapping the mini bar no longer snaps to the full player — the cover image animates
up from the bar to the centre of the screen, growing as it goes. The same animation
runs in reverse when the overlay is dismissed. The spring spec is tuned so the
motion feels responsive but settles cleanly, with no visible overshoot.

## Home-screen widget

A 4×1 widget showing the current track, album art, and play/pause + skip controls.
It updates through Glance, so it stays current without polling. The art is tinted
from the same palette as the app, so it matches the album even on the home screen.

## Music Assistant on ExoPlayer

The Sendspin player now runs on ExoPlayer with Oboe output, replacing the
hand-built decoder pipeline. This brings:

- Direct Hue light sync works for Music Assistant playback (the analysis tap sits
  in ExoPlayer's render chain)
- Volume follows what is actually making the sound — the hardware rocker reaches the
  player volume, not the phone's silent output
- Radio stations and podcasts in the library with the same motion as other categories
- Player settings live with the server: name, stream format, gapless, and what to
  ask the server for, all on the server's own card

## Smaller fixes

- **Album colours** were coming back faded because white pixels outvoted the actual
  colours they were a minority of
- **The seek bar** sawed backwards because position updates fought drag state
- **The Lights tab** rebuilt itself on every visit
- **The widget** read StateFlow.value in composition, so it never updated
