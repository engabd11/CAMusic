v0.11.0 - two more libraries, effects with real sound, and a room that stays in step
Stable build of CAMusic for Android.

The release that makes the features added over 0.10.x hold together: two more library
backends, ambience effects that sound like what they look like, and the timing fixes
that stop the lights drifting away from Music Assistant playback.

## Two more libraries

**Emby** and **Plex** join Navidrome, Subsonic, Jellyfin, Music Assistant and on-device
files. Both browse, play, download and scrobble.

Emby speaks an API near-identical to Jellyfin's with its own auth header, so it is its
own adapter rather than a flag on the Jellyfin one — the day the two diverge on a
capability, sharing one would have been a bug in one of them.

Plex never sees a password. Its account lives at plex.tv, not on the server the phone is
about to talk to, so signing in mints a PIN, opens plex.tv in the browser with the code
pre-filled, and polls until it comes back with a token. Genre browse filters by Plex's
own numeric tag id, which is what Plex takes — unlike Jellyfin and Subsonic, where the
genre's name is the key.

Every server kind now shows its own brand mark, everywhere a server is listed.

## Effects that sound like what they look like

Each bundled ambience effect now ships a real recorded bed, played by a parallel clip
player alongside the show. Effects whose sound has to line up with their lights keep the
synthesised sound, because a recording cannot be the show clock.

The show's audio moved to a process-scoped holder. It used to be a field on the Effects
screen's view model, which was fine by accident while every show was synthesised and
wrong the moment one was not: swiping the app away left a bed looping with nothing able
to reach it.

The master Light Sync switch and a running show also stopped fighting. A show that
self-opens the bridge session now says so, and stopping it puts the switch back only if
it was the one that turned it on. Two `action: start` PUTs on one entertainment area —
the state that poisons a Hue session for good — can no longer be raced into by tapping a
second effect while the first is still connecting.

New this release: a second thunderstorm, a real underwater bed, and shells that differ.

## A room that stays in step with Music Assistant

`AudioAnalysisTap` now reports how far behind the newest sample each analysed frame sits,
and `FrameDelayQueue` subtracts it. That one term is what makes it correct to *hold* the
backlog a `stream/start` burst leaves behind rather than chase it: the old servo consumed
at up to four times real time, running every envelope four times too fast for the best
part of a second after each burst, and then left the tap 40 ms from the end of audio the
speaker was still two and a half seconds from finishing — so the idle show painted over
the last two seconds of every track.

## Seen, not just heard

The visualiser reads as a spectrum now: the per-bin AGC that Light Sync needs is divided
back out, the bars run low to high instead of mirroring out from the centre, and the
ballistics are asymmetric with peak-hold caps, the way an analyser's are.

Lyrics read as typography. Settings are reorganised into Playback & Behavior, with the
card-in-card nesting gone.

## Fixes

- **Deleting downloads no longer blocks the UI thread.** Both halves of a delete are disk
  — the unlink and the Room write — and every caller outside the eviction loop is a tap
  handler. "Delete all downloads" over a filled cap was thousands of `unlink` calls and a
  full table wipe with the main thread waiting behind them.
- Light frames from every source are held by the same measure, and the palette no longer
  re-rolls mid-track.
- Resuming music from the idle show no longer flashes the room.
- An effect can no longer open a second bridge session, and the visualiser no longer
  freezes when one does.
- Beat brightness is smoothed on wall time, at a bounded rate.
- The minimised overlay player shows which speaker or player it is addressing.
- Leftover "Sendspin"/"Sendpin" strings are now CAMusic.
